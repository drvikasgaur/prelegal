## 4. Independent Contractor Relationship

The Contractor is an independent contractor and not an employee, partner or agent of the Client. The Contractor controls the manner and means of performing the Services and is responsible for all taxes, insurance and statutory contributions arising from the compensation paid under this Agreement. The Contractor is not entitled to any employee benefits from the Client.

## 5. Intellectual Property

All work product, deliverables and materials created by the Contractor specifically for the Client in performing the Services (the "Work Product") shall be the sole property of the Client upon full payment. The Contractor hereby assigns to the Client all rights, title and interest in the Work Product. The Contractor retains ownership of any pre-existing materials and general know-how, and grants the Client a non-exclusive, perpetual, royalty-free license to use any pre-existing materials incorporated into the Work Product.

## 6. Confidentiality

The Contractor shall keep confidential all non-public information of the Client obtained in connection with this Agreement, use it solely to perform the Services, and return or destroy it upon request or termination. This obligation survives termination of this Agreement.

## 7. Warranties

The Contractor warrants that (a) it has the right to enter into this Agreement, (b) the Services and Work Product will not infringe the intellectual property rights of any third party, and (c) it will comply with all applicable laws in performing the Services.

## 8. Termination

(a) Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience on {{notice_period_days}} days' written notice.
(b) Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately on written notice if the other party materially breaches it and fails to cure the breach within 10 days of notice.
(c) On termination, the Client shall pay the Contractor for Services performed up to the termination date, and the Contractor shall deliver all completed and in-progress Work Product.

## 9. Limitation of Liability

Except for breaches of confidentiality or intellectual property obligations, neither party is liable for any indirect, incidental or consequential damages, and each party's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid or payable under it.
